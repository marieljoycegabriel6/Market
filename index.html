How to Play / 遊戲指南

🎯 Goal: Build a balanced, healthy meal with a NT$ 500 budget based on Taiwan's official "My Plate" (我的餐盤) guidelines!

6 Major Food Groups / 六大類食物:

  • 🥛 Dairy / 乳品類: Milk, Yogurt (1-2 cups a day)
  • 🌾 Grains / 全穀雜糧類: Brown rice, Sweet potato, Oats
  • 🍗 Protein / 豆魚蛋肉類: Tofu, Fish, Eggs, Chicken
  • 🥦 Veggies / 蔬菜類: Spinach, Broccoli, Carrots
  • 🍎 Fruits / 水果類: Apples, Bananas, Guava
  • 🥜 Nuts & Seeds / 堅果種子類: Almonds, Walnuts, Sesame

⚠️ Watch out for sugary sodas, boba tea, and chocolates! They add high calories without essential nutrition.
